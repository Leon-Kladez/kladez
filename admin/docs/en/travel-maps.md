# Maps on your phone for the road

Kladez maps open on a phone only at home, over Wi-Fi, while the computer is on. On the road you need a navigator inside the phone. For that, Kladez gives you the free **Organic Maps** app: countries are downloaded to the phone, and it guides you by GPS without internet — by car, on foot or by bike. GPS works without internet and without a SIM card.

Organic Maps is free, open-source software built on the same OpenStreetMap data as Kladez maps. No ads, no sign-up, no tracking.

## Install the app on a phone (Android)

1. While there is internet, open Kladez on the computer → **Settings → [Phones](/settings/phones)** and click **Prepare the app for phones**. About 66 MB is downloaded, once.
2. Connect the phone to the same Wi-Fi as the computer.
3. Point the phone camera at the QR code in **Maps for the road on your phone** and tap the link. The file OrganicMaps.apk downloads.
4. Open the downloaded file. The phone asks whether apps from this source may be installed — allow it (for the browser you downloaded with) and tap **Install**.

No app store and no internet are needed for this — the app comes straight from the computer.

**iPhone:** install **Organic Maps** from the App Store while there is internet.

## Prepare for the trip

Organic Maps downloads the country maps themselves from the internet — do it in advance, at home:

1. Open Organic Maps, tap **menu → Download maps** and choose the countries or regions you will travel through (and neighbouring ones, for detours).
2. Check it: turn on **airplane mode**, open the map and build a route to any place. If the route is built, everything will work on the road.

Maps take from tens of megabytes (a small country) to several gigabytes (all of Russia) — check the free space on the phone.

## On the road

- Turn on **location (GPS)** — no internet needed.
- Find a place with search or tap the map, then **Route** — choose car, walking or bike.
- The navigator guides you with voice prompts and re-routes if you turn off.
- Bookmark important places (home, hospital, fuel) — they are stored on the phone.

## If it does not install

- **"Installation blocked"** — in the phone settings, allow installing apps from unknown sources for the browser you downloaded with.
- **The QR code does not open** — check that the phone is on the same Wi-Fi as the computer (not mobile data). See [Phones over Wi-Fi](/docs/phones).
- **It does not download on the computer** — internet is needed. Prepare the app when it is back.
