# Maps

Kladez can download a map of your country or region. After that the map works without internet: you can zoom in to individual streets and buildings, search for places and add markers.

## How to download a map

1. Open Settings → **[Maps Manager](/settings/maps)** (or the Maps step in [Easy Setup](/easy-setup)).
2. Click **Choose region** and find your country.
3. For Russia you can pick a federal district instead of the whole country — the map takes less space.
4. Choose how detailed the map should be:
   - **Detailed (down to buildings)** — streets and houses, needs more space;
   - **Overview (less space)** — towns and roads, no fine detail.
5. Kladez shows how much space the map needs and how much is free. Click **Download map**.

If the internet drops, the download continues when it is back.

A **search index** is downloaded together with a detailed map — it lets you find towns, streets, addresses and places without internet. If your map was downloaded before search existed, open the map, click the search box and then **Download search**.

## How to view the map

On the home page click the **Maps** tile. Drag the map with the mouse, zoom with the mouse wheel or the “+” and “−” buttons.

- **Search** — the box at the top: a town, a street and house number, a kind of place (“pharmacy”, “hospital”, “fuel”, “metro”) or its name. Places closer to the part of the map you are looking at come first. Coordinates work too: `55.75, 37.62`.
- **What's here?** — tap an icon or a building to see its name, kind and address, and add a marker from there.
- **Markers** — mark important places: a well, a pharmacy, a relative's house.
- **Icons** — pharmacies, hospitals, clinics, police, fire stations, metro, fuel, banks and more have their own icons; zoomed out, only the most important ones show. House numbers appear as you zoom in.

Outside the countries you downloaded, the map is blank or very rough — that is normal.

## On a phone

Phones at home can open the map over Wi-Fi — see [Phones over Wi-Fi](/docs/phones). For navigation on the road, without the computer, install the free **Organic Maps** app on your phone and download your region in it in advance.
