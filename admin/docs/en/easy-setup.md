# Easy Setup

Easy Setup helps you choose what to download into Kladez in a few minutes. Open it with the **Easy Setup** tile on the home page or here: **[Open Easy Setup →](/easy-setup)**

Everything you choose can be changed later: add or remove material, download more maps. You can run Easy Setup again at any time.

## Step 1. Language and apps

At the top, in large letters, is the language choice: **Русский / English**. Below it, tick what Kladez should do:

- **Information Library** — Wikipedia, medicine, textbooks, books. Almost everyone needs it.
- **AI assistant** — answers questions from your library. It needs a faster computer with 8 GB of memory or more. On a weak computer, better leave it out.
- **Education platform** — lessons with videos and exercises.

Click **Next**.

## Step 2. Maps

Pick the map of your country. For Russia you can pick a single federal district so the map takes less space. Details: [Maps](/docs/maps).

You can skip this step and download maps later.

## Step 3. Content — ready-made sets

The easiest way is to pick one of three ready-made sets. Their names are shown in Russian:

| Set | Disk space | What is inside |
|---|---|---|
| **Аптечка** (First Aid Kit) | about 5 GB | The essentials for a rainy day: medicine, popular Wikipedia articles with pictures, textbooks, an English survival shelf. |
| **Библиотека** (Library) | about 26 GB | All of Wikipedia without pictures, illustrated textbooks by subject, dictionaries and classic literature. |
| **Всё** (Everything) | about 62 GB | All of Wikipedia with pictures and everything else available in Russian, plus the full English shelf. |

Each set shows its exact size and whether it fits on your disk. Short on space? Start with the First Aid Kit — you can add more later.

Most of the material is in Russian. To choose by hand, use the detailed list by topic below the sets.

## Step 4. AI assistant

This step only appears if you chose the AI assistant. Pick a model — the assistant's “brain”. Bigger models give smarter answers but are slower and take more space. For an ordinary computer, pick a smaller one.

## Step 5. Review

Check what you selected and click **Complete Setup**.

## What happens next

Downloading starts and you can watch the progress.

- **You can close the page** — downloading continues on its own.
- **If the internet drops or the computer is switched off**, downloading resumes from the same place once everything is back.
- The sets are large: the First Aid Kit takes from half an hour to a few hours, Everything can take a day or more, depending on your connection.

While a set is downloading, the library may be unavailable or incomplete. That is normal.
