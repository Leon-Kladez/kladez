# About

**Kladez — a well of knowledge.** A free program in Russian (with an English interface too): a library of useful knowledge that is kept at home and works without internet.

## Why

The internet can disappear: an accident, a shutdown, a remote place. Yet knowledge increasingly lives only on other people's servers. Kladez keeps the most useful things — medicine, Wikipedia, textbooks, maps — at home, always at hand.

## Our rules

- **Free.** No ads, no paid “portions” of knowledge.
- **No tracking.** Kladez reports nothing about you to anyone. It only goes online to download the material you chose and to check for updates.
- **Open.** The program's code and the list of material are open: anyone can check what is inside.
- **Only what may be shared.** We use material that may be redistributed and record the licence of each source.
- **Neutral.** Useful knowledge only, no politics.

## Where the material comes from

Kladez does not host gigabytes of articles itself: it downloads them straight from open sources — mainly the [Kiwix](https://kiwix.org) catalog, which publishes Wikipedia and other material for offline reading. Maps are built from [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org) (© OpenStreetMap contributors).
