# Reading the library

The library is everything you downloaded: Wikipedia, medicine, textbooks, dictionaries, books. It is shown by Kiwix, which is built into Kladez.

## How to open it

On the Kladez home page click the **Read** tile (in Advanced mode it is called **Library**; it shows how many books and articles are downloaded). It opens in a new browser tab.

You will see a “shelf” — a card for each downloaded item: Wikipedia, Medicine and so on. Click a card to open it.

## How to find an article

- **Inside one item** — the search box at the top of the page. For example, open Wikipedia and type “burn”.
- **Across the whole library** — the search box on the shelf page.

Articles work like an ordinary website: follow the links, and the browser's Back button returns to the previous page.

{% callout type="warning" title="Medicine is not a doctor" %}
Medical articles help you understand what is happening and give first aid. If you can see a doctor or call an ambulance, do it.
{% /callout %}

## Language of the material

Most of the material in the Kladez catalog is in Russian. There is also an English shelf, mostly survival and repair guides that do not exist in Russian. If you want Wikipedia in your own language, look for it in the Content Explorer.

## Adding or removing material

- **Add:** the **[Download knowledge](/knowledge)** tile on the home page (or the **Add books** button on the library card). It has ready-made sets, Wikipedia and individual books by topic, with sizes. Even more is in Advanced mode: Settings → **[Content Explorer](/settings/zim/remote-explorer)**.
- **Remove:** in Advanced mode only: Settings → **[Content Manager](/settings/zim)**. Removing frees disk space.

## Where the files are

The library is stored on the drive you chose during installation, in the `Kladez\library` folder — for example `D:\Kladez\library`. Do not move or delete this folder by hand, or Kladez will no longer find it.
