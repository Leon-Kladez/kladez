# What is Kladez

Kladez (“a well of knowledge”) is a free knowledge library that lives at home, on this computer. While you have internet, it downloads useful material: Wikipedia, medicine, textbooks, books and maps. When the internet is gone, everything you downloaded still opens — on this computer and on phones at home over Wi-Fi.

Kladez does not report anything about you to anyone. There are no ads and nothing to pay for. Its code is open, so anyone can check what is inside.

## Where to start

1. On the home page, click the **[Library](/library)** tile. The medical Wikipedia is already there — it comes with Kladez.
2. Below, under **Add books**, pick a ready-made set («Аптечка» — First Aid Kit, «Библиотека» — Library, «Всё» — Everything) or open a topic and click **Download** next to a book. You can see how much space it needs and whether it fits.
3. Wait for the download: its row shows the percentage and the time left. Then the book appears in **My books** by itself — click **Read**. Details: [Reading the library](/docs/library).

## Simple and Advanced mode

At the top of the home page, on either side of the name, there are two buttons: **Simple** and **Advanced**.

- **Simple** (on by default) — only what most people need: Library, Maps, On your phone, Ask the assistant (if installed) and Help. There are no buttons here that can break anything.
- **Advanced** — everything else: the App depot, Settings, Easy Setup and your own links on the home page. If in doubt, switch back to Simple.

The mode is the same on every computer at home. Phones always see Simple mode.

## What Kladez has

- **Library** — Wikipedia, medicine, textbooks, dictionaries and books. [More](/docs/library)
- **Maps** — a map of your country or region that works offline. [More](/docs/maps)
- **AI assistant** — answers questions from your library. It runs on this computer, without internet. [More](/docs/ai)
- **Drug reference** — medicine labels (in English). [More](/docs/drug-reference)
- **Phones at home** — open the library and maps over Wi-Fi, nothing to install. [More](/docs/phones)
- **App depot** (in Advanced mode) — extra apps: notes, e-book reader, PDF tools and more. [More](/docs/supply-depot-apps)

## How to open Kladez

Double-click the **Kladez** shortcut on the desktop (its name is written in Russian: «Кладезъ»). Your browser opens the Kladez page. On this computer the address is `http://localhost:8080`.

Kladez starts by itself when the computer is switched on. The first start after power-on can take up to five minutes — if the page does not open right away, wait a little and click the shortcut again.
