# AI assistant

The AI assistant answers questions in plain language: “how to treat a burn”, “how to purify water”, “what is hypothermia”. It runs **on this computer, without internet**, and sends nothing anywhere.

## The main rule

The assistant answers from the material in your library and shows which article the answer came from. **Check the answer against the article.** AI can be wrong or mix things up — especially in medicine. The library itself is the main source; the assistant just helps you find things faster.

## How to turn it on

The library and maps do not need the assistant, so it is turned on separately:

1. In [Easy Setup](/easy-setup), tick **AI assistant**.
2. At the AI step, pick a model — the assistant's “brain”. For an ordinary computer, pick a smaller model: it answers faster and takes less space.
3. Wait for the model to download.

You can add or remove models later: Settings → the item with the assistant's name.

## How to ask

Click the round chat button in the bottom-right corner of the home page, or open **[Chat](/chat)**. Type your question and press Enter.

The assistant answers in the language of the question.

## Your own documents

The chat has a **Knowledge base** section: add your own files (PDF, text) and the assistant will answer from them too — for example, the manual for your boiler or family notes about medicines.

## If the assistant is slow

AI is the heaviest part of the program. On a weak computer an answer can take a minute or more.

- Pick a smaller model.
- Close other programs.
- It needs 8 GB of memory or more. With 4 GB the assistant will not work.
